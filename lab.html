HOW THE MACHINE WORKS · 2XM / 3XM / 4XM

From invisible wind to usable power.

At Senvion we are captivated by wind energy’s limitless potential and inspired to create a brighter future for future generations by utilising this invisible power, wind. Six systems turn moving air into grid-compliant electricity — click any of them in the model above, or raise the exploded view, to read the engineering data behind each one.

🌀

Rotor & blades

Three GFRP blades — 58.7 m, 63.7 m or 78.5 m — sweep 11,310 m² up to 20,106 m². Triple-row roller blade bearings cut friction at the root while the electrical pitch system feathers each blade to hold rated power or stop the machine.

⚙

Drivetrain

Main bearing, forged main shaft and a two-stage gearbox step roughly 10 rpm at the hub up to generator speed, with a high-speed shaft coupling and disc brake in between.

⚡

Generator & converter

DFIG with a frequency converter and DC link on the 2XM and 3XM machines, partial-converter DFIG on the 4XM — separate P/Q control, wide speed range, full Indian grid-code compliance.

🧭

Yaw & pitch

DC-motor pitch drives feather every blade independently while the yaw system keeps the nacelle square to the wind. Both are commanded by the condition-monitoring controller.

🕊

Tower & foundation

Tubular steel towers from 117 m to 140 m, plus a 170 m timber or concrete-hybrid option on the 4.2M160 — hub height tuned to the site’s wind shear.

📡

Control & FleetPro®

On-board SCADA streams every turbine to FleetPro® — state-of-the-art analytics, performance diagnostics and remote diagnostics that keep availability high across the fleet.
