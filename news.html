alfanar group Announces Additional direct capital funding of USD 100 Million (~INR 950 Crore) in Senvion to Accelerate Next Phase of Growth

Mumbai, India | August 25, 2026 – alfanar group today announced an additional direct capital funding of USD 100 million (~INR 320 Cr.) received in August 2026 and the rest of the direct capital funding to be completed in couple of weeks' time, to support the company's next phase of technology-led and manufacturing-driven growth.

The funding will support strategic priorities including development of Senvion's long-term product roadmap, growth capital to support and drive Senvion's growing business in India and global markets and accelerating the development of its project pipeline through the 'Senvion Certified Sites' offering.

The funding reinforces alfanar group's long-term commitment to the "Make in India" initiative and its confidence in India's emergence as a global hub for renewable energy manufacturing, innovation, and technology development.

Jamal Wadi, Chairman of Senvion and President of Alfanar Projects, said: "India is central to alfanar group's renewable energy strategy, and Senvion is a key part of our long-term vision for wind energy. This funding reflects our confidence in Senvion's leadership, engineering capabilities, and technology roadmap. By combining Senvion's wind engineering expertise with alfanar group's strengths in power systems, grid infrastructure, and energy storage, we are well positioned to develop the next generation of integrated renewable energy solutions and deliver long-term value for customers in India and international markets."

Shreyas B. Suvarna, CEO, Senvion, said: "We thank our shareholders and the Board for reaffirming their trust in Senvion. This funding strengthens the foundations of our business where it matters most. We will deepen our R&D capabilities to deliver more reliable and higher-performing turbine platforms, accelerate the introduction of new technologies, and enhance our ability to support customers throughout the 25-year lifecycle of their assets. We will also continue investing in our people by developing talent and creating a world-class workplace that combines leading market practices with alfanar group's globally recognized human capital excellence refined over 40 years. Our objective is clear: to build globally competitive technology platforms from India and become the partner of choice for customers, employees, and stakeholders across India and international markets."

About Senvion India

Senvion is one of the leading wind turbine manufacturers in India, with operations in the country since 2016. The company has established a strong presence through a world-class in-house R&D setup, a manufacturing capacity of ~1.5 GW/annum, and an operational fleet exceeding 1.6 GW nationwide.

Senvion plays a key role in the development and execution of wind energy projects across the country, with a strong focus on innovation, localisation, specialised project development and sustainability. The company has deployed advanced turbine technologies, including 120m, 130m, and 160m rotor platforms, with power ratings ranging from 2.3 MW to 4.2 MW, designed to optimise performance for global wind regimes.

Committed to clean energy and sustainable growth, Senvion continues to support India's transition to a greener future.

alfanar and Senvion Reaffirm Commitment to Localisation and 'Make in India' at Powering The Next Era

Mumbai, India | June 30, 2026: At Powering The Next Era, a leadership and stakeholder engagement event hosted recently in Mumbai, alfanar and Senvion have reaffirmed their commitment to strengthening India's domestic wind energy ecosystem.

The event brought together customers, partners, senior leadership from alfanar and Senvion, and key stakeholders from both companies. The discussions highlighted the company's continued focus on developing wind turbine solutions that are designed, engineered, and manufactured in India. These solutions are also supported throughout their operational lifecycle. The approach aligns with the broader vision of building solutions "in India, for India and global markets."

With this renewed focus, Senvion has also marked a new phase for the wind turbine OEM under the leadership of Shreyas B. Suvarna, CEO, Senvion India.

Shreyas B. Suvarna, newly appointed CEO of Senvion India, stated, "India has built a strong manufacturing base. The next step is to move up the value chain by strengthening design and innovation capabilities. At Senvion, this transition is already underway, supported by our R&D teams in India and Germany. Our focus is to design and build in India, for both domestic and global markets, while ensuring long-term reliability and lifecycle support. This is key to positioning India as a global hub for wind energy innovation."

Jamal Wadi, Chairman of Senvion and President of alfanar, added, "India is an important market in the global renewable energy transition. Senvion's growth reflects our long-term commitment to building strong local capabilities. As part of alfanar's renewable energy and manufacturing ecosystem, the company is well-positioned to support India's wind energy value chain through technology, scale, and execution strength. We will continue to invest in localisation and capability building to support India's clean energy ambitions."

Senvion became part of alfanar's global renewable energy ecosystem in 2021, following its full acquisition. The acquisition was carried out through Global Renewable Energy Development Holding Company Limited (GREDHCL), an entity established by alfanar's promoters. This integration has enabled Senvion to leverage alfanar's capabilities across renewable energy solutions, EPC, electrical systems, grid infrastructure, battery energy storage systems (BESS), sustainable fuels, and industrial manufacturing, while deepening its local presence in India.

The company's turbines are manufactured in India and supported by in-house R&D teams based in India and Germany, currently operating a manufacturing capacity of approximately 1.5 GW per annum, with facilities across Tamil Nadu, Maharashtra, and Gujarat. To date, Senvion has installed around 1.7 GW of wind energy capacity, of which nearly 1.6 GW is operational.

The foundation of Senvion as an independent India-focused wind business.

ACQUISITION OF SENVION GMBH'S INDIA OPERATIONS

3rd December 2020, Germany/Mumbai: Senvion GmbH has entered into contractual agreements for the sale of 100% of its Indian operations ("Senvion India") on 01 December 2020. Senvion had previously ringfenced the Senvion India business, which is a full-fledged Wind OEM and in the business of WTG manufacturing, turnkey EPC solutions with project development, O&M; and Asset Management, from its self-administration process.

The transaction envisages the transfer of complete ownership of Senvion India along with all assets such as the factory, installed base of O&M; and current ongoing projects, intellectual property rights relevant for the Indian market and its strong 300+ team size.

The acquiring entity, GREDHCL (Global Renewable Energy Development Holding Company Limited) is a fund based out of the Dubai International Financial Centre and subject to regulatory approvals, the sale is proposed to close in Q1-2021. EY acts as financial advisor to GREDHCL and Rothschild & Co acts as financial advisors to Senvion GmbH in this transaction.

Amit Kansal, CEO and MD of Senvion India, said: "Senvion India has been a key participant in the Indian Renewable energy sector since its inception in 2016. Senvion aligned itself to the government goals of localization and Make-in-India at a very early stage and now makes more than 85% of its turbine parts in India thus generating local employment and innovations. Senvion firmly believes that Wind Power generation is critical in the energy mix to achieve the 450 GW of renewable power by 2030. Senvion India is committed to relentlessly work to develop innovative technologies to attain the goals of delivering competitive wind power."

About GREDHCL

GREDHCL is a DIFC-UAE based entity and its Promoters are seasoned professionals with decades of experience in energy infra sector. They own & manage multiple businesses in MENA, Europe, UK and Asia in the field of electrical manufacturing, project management & development and O&M services.
