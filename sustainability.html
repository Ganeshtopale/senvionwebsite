09 — SUSTAINABILITY · OUR COMMITMENT TO A SUSTAINABLE TOMORROW

Let's drive the future of clean energy, together.

At Senvion India, sustainability is at the core of our mission. We engage with diverse stakeholders to identify ESG-related material topics, drawing insights from customer surveys, industry trends and regulatory agencies. In 2022 we appointed CareEdge Advisory to integrate ESG principles into our processes and conduct our first materiality assessment, reviewed by our ESG Executive Oversight Committee.

🔍 ESG evaluation & gap analysis

We identified areas for enhancing and developing the organisation's ESG practices with the help of a comprehensive ESG gap analysis.

⚖️ Materiality assessment

We determined ESG priorities through a materiality exercise and aligned them with relevant ESG Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) and targets.

📈 Peer comparison

We gained insights into the ESG practices of our peers and developed a clear roadmap and strategy for Senvion through careful peer benchmarking.

🗂️ Data collection & calculation

We facilitated the collection of ESG data by providing templates and supported the calculation of emissions, as evidenced in the ESG report.

Diversity and equal opportunity, LTIFR safety targets, compliance and business-ethics training, and a Supplier Code of Conduct across Tier 1 & 2 vendors.
